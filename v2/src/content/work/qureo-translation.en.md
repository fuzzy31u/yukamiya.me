## Protect the learning experience

Translation is part of product delivery. Correct data structures alone do not establish whether a lesson works for a child; visual and editorial review remain important.

## Choose for the task

Model quality, preparation cost, and ongoing usage cost all belong in the decision. The newest model is not automatically the right choice.
